IBM Partner · IBM Confluent specialists
Why it matters

With IBM’s acquisition of Confluent, the data-in-motion platform and Brazil’s critical legacy ecosystem (MQ, mainframe, Cloud Pak, Event Streams) now sit under one roof. We are IBM partners specialized in IBM Confluent: managed Kafka, Flink, Schema Registry, Connect and Tableflow, in the cloud or on-prem.

What we do together
  • Deployment and operations of Confluent Cloud, Platform and Private Cloud
  • Mainframe and MQ decoupling through CDC and events
  • Our own Event Streams → Confluent migration framework
  • Co-sell and events: Kafka Meetup SP, Data In Motion, IBM Think and Tech Summit

Developer Experience partner
Why it matters

Lenses is the developer experience layer for Kafka: a single interface to explore topics, query data with SQL, catalog schemas and enforce governance (RBAC, auditing, masking) across many clusters at once, whether Confluent, MSK or open-source Kafka. It cuts the reliance on scripts and speeds up teams building on events.

What we do together
  • Cluster observability and troubleshooting with SQL Studio and alerts
  • Governance: data catalog, RBAC, auditing and masking policies
  • Multi-cluster operations with Lenses 6 and per-environment agents
  • Enablement for development teams
